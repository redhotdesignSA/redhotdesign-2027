      What is actually going on

      It was built to look right, not to do a job.

      Nearly every website we are asked to fix was signed off on how it looked. Whether it would bring in business was never part of the conversation.

      What was agreed

      • Colours and fonts
      • How the pages look
      • Which photographs to use
      • How many pages there would be
      • A date to go live

      Questions

      Plain answers

      Do you build new websites from scratch?

      Yes. Web design is where we started in 2008, and we have built more than 500 websites since. New businesses and startups are welcome. If you have no website at all, we design and build one properly from the first page.

      Do you fix or rebuild websites you did not build?

      That is a large part of the work. Typically the content is still sound, but the site is dated, slow or hard to update. We keep what works and rebuild the rest.

      How long does a website take?

      It depends on the size. For an established company site of twenty to sixty pages, six weeks from kickoff to live is normal. You approve it in stages as it goes up, so there is no long silence followed by one reveal.

      Who actually does the work?

      A small senior team, and the person on your first call stays on your project.

      How does this sit with POPIA?

      Every tracking, consent and form setup we install is built to comply with POPIA from the start. All data stays in accounts you own.

      Four questions, and then we go and look at your website properly. You get a plain written answer on what is holding it back and what we would do first, whether or not you take it any further.
