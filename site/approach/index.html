    Approach

    No discovery calls. We look first.

    By the time we speak we have been through your site and, where you have shared access, your ad accounts. The first conversation is about findings, not introductions.

    01 · If it is the spend

    We audit before we touch a single campaign.

    01

    Read-only access, one week

    Google Ads, Meta, Analytics and your CRM if you have one. Read-only is enough. Nothing changes while we look, so there is no risk to a campaign that is currently working.

    02

    Findings, in writing

    Where the budget is going, what is being counted twice, what is being counted not at all, and what the landing pages are doing to the traffic you already pay for. Written so that a CFO can read it, with the account screenshots behind every claim.

    03

    You decide who executes

    Your agency can take the findings and run with them. We are happy with that outcome and we will brief them properly. If you would rather we did it, we scope and quote it. Either way you keep the audit.

    04

    Then monthly, against revenue

    Restructure, test, report. Measured against closed revenue from your CRM rather than platform-reported conversions, because the two rarely agree and only one of them pays salaries.

    02 · If it is the site

    We read the whole thing before we design anything.

    01

    Everything gets read

    Every page, product sheet, PDF and accreditation. We build an inventory of what the business actually says about itself, which is routinely more than the current site manages to show.

    02

    Structure agreed, once

    One short conversation about sitemap and priority. We keep your existing structure unless there is a commercial reason to change it, so your team is not relearning their own website.

    03

    Built and staged in sections

    Sections appear on a staging URL as they are finished. You comment, we adjust, we move on. No eight-week silence ending in one nervous reveal, and no surprise invoice for a direction nobody agreed to.

    04

    Live, then maintained

    Launch is a day, not a finish line. Changes continue at the same rhythm afterwards, which is how a site stays current instead of ageing quietly into the next rebuild.

    We use AI heavily in our own production, which is why a sixty-page corporate rebuild takes weeks rather than months. Every line is reviewed by a person before it reaches your staging site, and nothing is published that we have not read.

    Four questions, then we look.

    You get a written view of what we would do first, whether or not you take it further.
