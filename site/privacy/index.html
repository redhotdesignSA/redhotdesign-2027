    Privacy notice

    How we handle your information

    Last updated 16 November 2026. This notice explains how RedHot Design CC (registration 2008/189710/23), of Gqeberha, Eastern Cape, South Africa, collects and uses personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

    What we collect

    When you complete the enquiry form on this website we collect the name, company, work email address, and optionally the mobile number, website address and any notes you choose to give us, together with your answers to the scoping questions. We also record the page you submitted from and the website that referred you.

    We store a one-way cryptographic hash of your IP address rather than the address itself, so that we can detect abuse without holding information that identifies your location or connection.

    Why we collect it

    Solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you choose to work with us, to deliver the work. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with advertising platforms, and we do not add you to a marketing list without you asking us to.

    Your consent

    The enquiry form requires you to tick a box confirming that we may use your details to reply. That consent is recorded with your enquiry. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing hello@redhotdesign.co.za, and we will delete your record.

    Where it is stored

    Enquiries are stored on our hosting account in South Africa, outside the public web root, and are not accessible from the internet. Notification email is delivered through Brevo, our transactional email provider. Access is limited to the people at RedHot Design who need it to reply to you.

    How long we keep it

    Enquiries that do not become work are kept for twenty-four months and then deleted. Records relating to clients are kept for as long as the law requires us to keep business records, and no longer.

    Your rights under POPIA

    You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@redhotdesign.co.za and we will respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

    Cookies and analytics

    This website sets no cookies at all until you agree to them. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking on this site under any circumstances.

    We ask once, in a bar at the bottom of the page, whether we may measure how the site is used. If you decline, or simply ignore it, nothing is loaded and nothing is stored. Your choice is remembered in your own browser so that we do not ask again.

    If you agree, we load Google Analytics 4 to count visits, see which pages are read and understand how people arrive. It runs with Google Consent Mode and IP anonymisation enabled. We use it to improve this website. We do not use it to build advertising audiences, we do not enable Google Signals, and we do not share this data with any advertising platform.

    You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser, which removes the stored choice and makes us ask again.

    The enquiry form

    The form is processed on our own server. It records your submission before any email is sent, so that an email failure cannot lose your enquiry. The form stores no raw IP address, only a one-way hash, and sets no cookies.

    Client work

    Where we build or manage websites, advertising accounts or measurement for a client, the client is the responsible party for their own visitors' information and we act as an operator on their instruction. The tracking, consent and form setups we install are built to comply with POPIA, including the Section 69 rules on direct marketing.
